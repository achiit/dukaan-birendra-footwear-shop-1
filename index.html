Fifty years, one shop, one promise

I started this shop on Beleghata Road in 1976 with a few pairs and a lot of hope. Back then my uncle used to sit beside me and teach me how to judge leather by touch and smell.

Today people bring their children and even grandchildren here. Some still show me the pair they bought from us twenty years ago and ask me to polish it. That is the best compliment I can get.

I do not push any pair. I will tell you honestly if a shoe suits your foot or not.
